## note: building-blocks

Plot is designed around a simple workflow that keeps your focus where you choose while knowing
what's happening.

New and updated threads arrive at the top of **Active**. _When a thread…_

- _… is just FYI_: Simply read it and it'll move to **Done**.
- _… needs follow-up or careful reading_: Mark it **To do** to keep it in **Active**.
- _… deserves attention later_: Mark it **Do later** to move it out of **Active** until a future
  day.
- _… belongs in another focus_: **Move** it. Plot learns and moves similar threads there in the
  future.
- _… never needs attention_: **Mute** it. You can always find threads in Done or with search, but
  muted threads (and similar ones) will skip **Active**.

Instead of interruptions fragmenting your day, you can trust that everything gets done at the best
time.

## note: focuses

By bringing all you work together, Plot eliminates time and attention lost to jump around tools.
Instead of organizing your work by _where_ it is, you organize it by _how_ you work on it. This lets
you make real progress in an area you choose.

Create focuses for:

- _Important projects_: With everything related to a project in one focus, you can find what you
  need quickly, with a clear view of what's outstanding. Even better, you can send related messages
  and replies without getting pulled into unrelated work that happens to be in the same chat, inbox,
  or app.
- _Core responsibilities_: At work, you might have direct responsibilities while also managing
  others. Putting these in separate focuses ensures you make progress on your goals while giving
  your team the attention they deserve.
- _Key activities_: Certain activities are done best with concentrated focus, like customer and
  sales conversations, or reviewing progress from a large team. In a focus, you choose how much and
  how often to invest your time.

Focuses aren't just for your most important priorities. By creating focuses for low-urgency
activities like unsolicited requests, you can batch them to complete efficiently less frequently.

Focuses are grouped within roles. Add a role for each areas of your life, such as work, personal,
and volunteering. Roles help you stay on top of commitments that easily get buried by your biggest
role.

## note: notifications

**Notifications** let you choose when updates in a focus should interrupt you based on how quickly
you need know about them. For many, your best move is to turn notifications off and schedule the
right time for the focus. For others, set how quickly and the times they are sent to maintain your
desired response time. While you can get an immediate notification for everything, every time you
choose to silence or batch notifications, you gain focus for what is more important each day.
