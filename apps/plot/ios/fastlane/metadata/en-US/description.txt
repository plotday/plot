Your most important work isn't the newest email or the loudest notification. It's scattered across a dozen apps, waiting on you. Plot brings it together so you can see what needs you and make real progress on it.

Plot pulls every conversation that needs a human reply — email, team chat, and the comment threads inside the tools you already use — into one place, organized around the roles and goals you care about. Reply, react, assign, and finish work right where it lands, without opening five apps full of distractions.

Each day holds the same number of hours. Plot helps you invest them in the work that matters, and keeps everything else from spending them for you.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Bring email, team chat, meeting notes, and app comments into one organized list
• Reply, react, comment, and change status from Plot — it posts back through the original tool
• Mark anything To do, schedule it for later, or finish it — so nothing gets dropped
• Group your work under roles and focuses, so the right things get your best attention
• Let low-signal mail — newsletters, receipts, promotions — wait in a muted FYI focus instead of pinging you
• Set when interruptions are allowed; genuinely urgent threads still break through
• See your whole day — calendar events and scheduled work — on one agenda
• Search across everything, wherever it came from
• Chat with Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini right alongside your work — or turn AI off entirely

WORKS WITH YOUR TOOLS
Connect Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Chat, Outlook Calendar, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Linear, Notion, PostHog, and Apple Calendar. WhatsApp, Instagram, and LinkedIn messaging are available too. Read and reply in one place; jump to the source whenever you need it.

WORKS OFFLINE
Read, write, organize, and finish work with no connection — everything syncs the moment you're back online, across your iPhone, iPad, Mac, and the web.

WITH PEOPLE, NOT AROUND THEM
Great work happens with people. Share a Plot thread with anyone for free, bring the right people in, and move work forward together — no per-seat fees, no history limits.

YOUR CALL
Choose your AI model or turn it off entirely. Bring your own key. Your data stays yours — connections use standard OAuth, data is encrypted in transit, and access is enforced at the database level.

Plot is free to start. Get back to the work only you can do.
