Make progress on your priorities by organizing all yours tasks, documents, messages, and meetings in one focused workspace. When you choose a focus, you have the context you need to move forward with what matters.

<b>PEAK PRODUCTIVITY</b>

• <b>Unified Timeline</b>: See all your activity and upcoming actions in a unified timeline
• <b>Zoom In, Zoom Out</b>: Your areas of focus are nested so you can zoom right into a specific priority, or zoom out to see everything
• <b>Cross-Platform</b>: Seamlessly sync across iOS, macOS, Android, and web (with Windows coming soon)
• <b>Work Offline</b>: All your information is available offline, so you can stay productive without an internet connection

<b>... WITH A TWIST</b>

• Add Plot Twists to integrate with your favorite apps and services and automate your workflows
• Use pre-built Twists or create your own
• Popular Twists include integrations with calendar apps, task managers, AI chat, note-taking apps, and communication tools

We all have more to do than time to do it. Grinding through endless to-do lists only keeps us from our most important work. Plot is designed to focus you on what matter most while ensuring you don't drop the ball on anything else.
