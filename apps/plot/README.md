## Updating Drift

Two Drift dependancies are copied into the `web` folder:

1. [sqlite3](https://github.com/simolus3/sqlite3.dart/releases) `sqlite3.wasm`
1. [Drift](https://github.com/simolus3/drift/releases) `drift_worker.dart.js`
